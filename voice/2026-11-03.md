**Spencer:** how is the choosing of Representative different from the Senate to the Congress

**Bingo:** Representatives are elected by the voters of a single district, every two years. Each state gets seats based on its population, so California has fifty-two and the smallest states have one. Senators are elected by the voters of the whole state, every six years. Every state gets exactly two, no matter its size, and only about a third of the Senate is up in any one election.

One bit of history: until 1913, state legislatures picked senators. The seventeenth amendment switched that to a direct popular vote.
