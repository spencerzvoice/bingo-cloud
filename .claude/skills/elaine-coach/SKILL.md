## Elaine's 5 steps (Review Worksheet, Elaine Craig Voice Casting)
1. **Last line first** (disclaimers don't count) — often sets the tone; does it tie to the first line or tie the copy points together?
2. **Set apart the first line** — take your time; most important line; show inflection/attitude that you get the concept.
3. **Reformat for conversational flow** — remove ellipses, periods→commas, group lines, add contractions/parentheses; notice repeated words/phrases (rhythm).
4. **Find transitions** — problem→solution; tone/pace shifts subtly at the turn. Anthem copy usually breaks into 3 parts.
5. **Paraphrase the main point in one sentence** — what it offers, how it helps the listener.

## Elaine's standing rules
- Casting asks 3 things: believable (lived it; research the brand's existing tone), relatable, creative/unique (imagine what everyone does on line 1 → differentiate).
- Post-COVID: entertaining, "we both know this" common ground. "Slick" = going through the motions.
- Radio: close ellipsis gaps, no dead air, hit spec time but vary tempo inside; read straight first, improv after. TV: pictures carry story → more intimate. Radio needs more personality.
- Healthcare: white coat → bring it home to someone you care about. "Conversational" → "thoughtful", slow down. Same warm person through legal, just faster.
- Anthem ≠ big; = what the brand stands for. Support the picture, don't oversell. Spread multi-word direction across specific lines. Luxury: don't rush, hold last word of sentences.
- Casual/food: youthful not slick; linger on descriptors; build quiet open → discovery → lighter shrug end.
- **Always give an alternate** (flat/deadpan "here's the deal").
- Vary pace/emotion line by line; lean into the copywriter's chosen words; connect via transition words.
- Editing: invisible, natural; don't over-edit breaths; ride levels.

## Spencer-specific (from Elaine, 2026-09-19)
Deep voice already = commanding/confident → **don't reach for it; go warm, soften.** Imagine a specific listener (sleeping baby; bedtime story with a smile). Don't attack each line. Connect line-to-line via transitions — avoid choppy.

## Output format (terse)
For each script:
1. **Type + medium** (radio/TV/digital; humor/healthcare/anthem/casual…) and the applicable Elaine rule.
2. **5-step breakdown** — last line, first line, reformat (show the actual marked-up edits), transitions (where tone shifts), one-sentence point.
3. **Who you're talking to** + a concrete imagery/memory anchor.
4. **Take 1 (main)** and **Take 2 (alternate, flat/deadpan or different POV)** — direction per line/section, not one blanket adjective.
5. **Watch-outs for Spencer** (warmth, choppiness, over-authority).
6. **Elaine source** — which notes/transcript line each key call comes from, or "my read."
