## Step 1 — Pick the video (per client)

**LATEST VIDEO = PART OF DISCOVERY, NEVER WAIT TO BE ASKED (Spencer, 2026-10-13: "Finding the latest video is a part of the discovery ... you don't need me to prompt you to find the latest video this is already a part of your task and your job").** Every hook search and every re-voice pick runs `python scripts/latest_video_check.py "Funnel X" [Client]` (FGAC key in `K`) and judges EVERY `CHECK` line it prints (frames + transcript + `narrator_check.py`) before keeping a pick or writing a hook; the newest upload is often both the hook and the re-voice (Confluent VCARB F1 spot, Oct 6: two runs saw it and kept a 2025 video). Verdicts go in VIDEO-PICKS.md; a better pick = REVOICE SWAP + rebuild in the same session. A hook pass that skipped this is not done.
**LATEST LAUNCH VIDEO FIRST (Spencer, 2026-10-08: "always the latest technology or the latest video that they've released").** For any tech or product company (anything that ships products, features or releases), the pick is the video for its **latest** launch or release. Work back from the newest launch: if the newest launch videos fail the narrator rules (e.g. Apple's 10-08 iPhone 18 Pro / iPhone Duo / Watch Series 12 1-minute films are music-only, with no VO), try that launch's other videos ("Introducing…", overview, feature film), then the next most recent launch. Never jump to an older generic brand or explainer video while a newer narrated launch video exists. Report the launch and its date on the WINNER line. The age gate below still applies; this rule is stricter (newest launch, not just "under 3 years").
**STRICTLY VO OVER VISUALS (Spencer, 2026-10-03): an off-screen narrator over motion graphics / product UI / b-roll, with no on-screen person speaking at all.** Best genre = product and brand films like the Funnel C-F picks (Snowflake, Retool, Zendesk Copilots, Intercom Fin 2, Dropbox Sign). Acted TV and Super Bowl spots with people talking on camera are the wrong genre (Funnel G's mistake). Scripted, single narrator, ~15–125 s. Product overviews with VO over UI/graphics are GOOD. Reject tutorials, webinars, screen-recorded walkthroughs with a presenter, interviews/panels/podcasts, keynotes, testimonial montages, talking-head, music-only sizzles, anything ≳2 min. Website-embedded marketing videos outrank generic channel uploads.
**NO VO THAT BELONGS TO A WOMAN ON SCREEN (Spencer, 2026-10-02, hard rule).** A female *announcer* narrator is fine. Reject: (a) a woman on camera as the talking head / presenter / speaking lead; (b) a female VO that is the on-screen woman's own voice: her inner monologue, her first-person thoughts, "I/my" lines while she's the character we follow. Spencer's male bass/baritone replacing a woman's own voice is awkward. (Funnel G shipped Coinbase "15 bank visits": a woman on camera, first-person VO.) Talking heads and lip-sync of any gender stay rejected. **Read `reference/client-acquisition/VIDEO-PICKS.md` (Spencer's good/bad verdicts) before picking, and log every verdict there.** Gate every winner with `scripts/narrator_check.py`. MALE = fine. FEMALE or UNCLEAR = look at the contact sheet and read the script: if a woman is the on-screen protagonist AND the VO is first-person / her thoughts, reject and take the next candidate; an off-screen announcer voice is OK.
**MALE-VOICE FIT (Spencer, 2026-10-07, lululemon blissfeel: "a woman narration talking about women, and a man's voice over this would just clash"):** reject any spot whose narration is written by/for/about women (women's lines, "she/her/a woman's", female-athlete stories) or where a woman is the lead the VO speaks for. A gender-fit FLAG is a reject, not a maybe: never ship a flagged pick for Spencer to discover.
**PICTURE CHECK (Spencer, 2026-10-07, after SoFi shipped a Josh Allen talking head: "make sure you're doing better picking videos"):** `narrator_check.py` only hears the voice; it cannot see an on-camera speaker. Before any pick is final: pull a frame every 2 s (`ffmpeg -i src.mp4 -vf fps=0.5,scale=480:-1 frames/%03d.jpg`), LOOK at the frames (Read tool), and line them up with the Whisper timestamps. Any span where the narration plays while a person on screen talks, lip-syncs or addresses camera = reject. Celebrity/athlete endorsement spots are on-camera by default; assume reject until the frames prove otherwise. Report the picture-check result per candidate.
**Swapping a client's video = replace ALL of it in one pass (Spencer, 2026-10-03):** new source + NOVOX + Ableton project (`--check` 0 broken), the `<Client>_script` doc rewritten with the NEW copy (verify by exporting it), the `sources` doc headed with the new URL, a superseding entry in the funnel's `_candidates` doc, the draft's re-voice title, and a VIDEO-PICKS.md row. Move old files to `_rejected - <reason>/`, **including the old `<Client>_script` and `sources` docs (.docx AND .gdoc)**: `revoice_build.py`'s `has_script()` counts any `_script.gdoc` as "script exists" and silently skips Whisper (Psyop 10-07: Spencer opened the folder and found no script). After the build, open `<Client>_script.docx` and confirm it holds the NEW narration before saying it's ready. Then give Spencer the new script inline in chat so he isn't hunting for it. Spencer hand-picked the Funnel B/C sources; the routine (below) scrapes for new funnels.

## Step 2 — Download
```
python -m yt_dlp -f "bestvideo[height<=1080][vcodec^=avc1]+bestaudio[ext=m4a]/bestvideo[height<=1080]+bestaudio/best[height<=1080]/best" --merge-output-format mp4 --restrict-filenames -o "<dest>/%(title)s [%(id)s].%(ext)s" "<url>"
```
Unlisted playlists need their `&si=` token. Route by the video's YouTube **channel name → client folder** (matched every client). Write the URL to `sources.txt`.

## Step 3 — Strip the vocals → `<Client>_NOVOX.mp4`
Offline via **Demucs** (replaces lalal.ai; Funnel B was originally split with lalal.ai — those files are `*_no_vocals_split_by_lalalai.mp4`).
```
ffmpeg -i "<Client> - SOURCE - <title>.mp4" -vn -ac 2 -ar 44100 audio.wav
python -m demucs --two-stems=vocals -o stems audio.wav        # -> stems/htdemucs/audio/no_vocals.wav
ffmpeg -i "<Client> - SOURCE - <title>.mp4" -i stems/htdemucs/audio/no_vocals.wav -map 0:v:0 -map 1:a:0 -c:v copy -c:a aac -shortest "<Client>_NOVOX.mp4"
```
**Name the source file `<Client> - SOURCE - <title>.mp4` right after download, before Step 5, and upload it under exactly that name.** The `.als` links by filename. On 2026-09-30, 11 of 18 Funnel G projects were built from a scratch `src.mp4` that was renamed on upload, which left ORIGINAL VIDEO pointing at a file that didn't exist. `cloud_ableton.py` now refuses any other name. Check or repair a funnel on desktop with `python cloud_ableton.py --check "D:/My Drive/Client Outreach/<Funnel>" [--fix]`. A package isn't done until that prints 0 broken.
Picture untouched (`-c:v copy`); audio = music/SFX bed only. ~15–60 s/clip on CPU after the one-time ~80 MB model download. Spot-check for ghosting on dense mixes. Skip files that are already music-only (e.g. `MasterClass_Background_Music.mp4`).

## Step 4 — Script → `<Client>_script.docx` (**.docx, not .txt** — Spencer, 2026-09-24; python-docx, one paragraph per block)
- YouTube: `python -m yt_dlp --write-subs --write-auto-subs --sub-langs "en.*" --sub-format vtt --skip-download -o "%(id)s.%(ext)s" <url>` → strip timestamps/tags/dupes → punctuate and paragraph. Prefer human subs over auto.
- No captions (e.g. iSpot download): `whisper audio.wav --model small --output_format txt` (16 kHz mono wav; ffmpeg must be on PATH).
- **Lay it out as heard (Spencer, 2026-10-14: "They're sentences that are run on and should be chopped up. Pay attention to the pauses and the audio, and present the lines as they are heard ... no weird spaces").** Build every new script with `scripts/script_lines.py` (`--audio <vocal stem> --video <source> --out <docx> --client <brand>`, `--text <captions>` to keep caption words). **Layout v2 (Spencer, 2026-10-28: "weird formatting issues, random indentations and spacing that is distracting"):** the pause-only breaker cut mid-phrase ("just caught / up with", "December 22, / 1941") and let 70+ character lines wrap one word onto its own line, so it read as random indents. Now: a line ends at every sentence end and at clause punctuation; a clause over 68 characters splits at the narrator's longest pause, preferring the spot before "and/because/where/if/with...", never after "the/a/to/just", never inside a name or number ("Kimi K3.1 Pro", "December 22, 1941"); 1" margins, Arial 14, the same 6 pt after every line, a small grey header. `script_lines.py --lint` must print FORMAT OK: it now fails long lines that wrap, mid-phrase breaks, split dates, and any indent, tab or uneven spacing in the docx itself (the old lint passed every Funnel S script). `revoice_build.py`'s gate reports FORMAT issues.
- **Desktop words = Whisper large-v3 (faster-whisper), not medium (Spencer, 2026-10-28: "you're missing words on some scripts in Funnel S. With Funnel S1 and S2 make sure your scripts are 100% correct").** `script_lines.py` defaults to large-v3 wherever faster-whisper is installed, and `revoice_build.whisper_script()` takes the words from the channel's own human YouTube captions when it has them (large-v3 then only places the line breaks). To fix an existing script's words: `scripts/script_rebuild.py "<client folder>" --text <corrected.txt> [--note "[... don't voice it.]"]` (corrected = the large-v3 transcript, `--print-transcript`, fixed where the strict check's ears agree, names spelled from the video's own description). It keeps the original in `_rejected - old script <date>/` and never overwrites that original on a re-run.
- First line of the file: `Original copy - from captions, verify against the video.` (or `... Whisper transcription ...`).
- Spencer also gets one `.docx` per spot emailed to himself ("Funnel B Copy" / "Funnel C Copy") — minimal hand-rolled OOXML zips (~1.5 KB each; python-docx output is ~35 KB and too big to inline as base64 attachments).
- Don't script multi-speaker documentary/interview pieces (Big Think dropped for this reason) — not re-voiceable.
- **The file must contain the actual spoken VO copy, word for word. Never a summary, a description of the ad, "context", or a "transcribe manually" note (Spencer, 2026-09-24).** Funnel D shipped three scripts with no script in them: Experian (a summary of the ad, with the lines deliberately left out; wrong, because Spencer needs the exact copy to re-voice his own sample), Snowflake and Tendril ("transcription failed — transcribe manually"). If captions are missing or blocked, run Whisper locally (`--model medium` for music-heavy spots). A download or transcription failure is a FAIL for that client, not a placeholder file.
- **Script gate (run before reporting any package done):** open every `_script.docx` and confirm:
  1. The body is the narrator's lines, in order.
  2. The word count is plausible for the video length (≈1.5–3 words/sec of VO).
  3. There is no "NO CAPTIONS", "transcribe manually", "summary", "structure above" or similar placeholder text.
  4. `scripts/script_check.py` prints **SCRIPT CHECK OK** for it (`python script_check.py "<client folder>"`, or `--src <video> --text <script.docx/.txt>`). It runs a fresh word-timestamped Whisper pass over *that* source video (condition_on_previous_text off, nothing past the video's end, stock "thanks for watching" filler dropped) and diffs it against the script. It FAILS on any run of 3+ narrated words missing (a dropped line or tag: Airbnb, 10-12), 3+ script words not in the video (wrong video, paraphrase) or a match under 0.85. A small-model miss is re-heard on medium before it counts, so 1-2 word mishearings ("appraise" = après) never fail. `[Bracketed notes]` aren't read as copy; an on-camera line quoted inside a `[Not narrator: "..."]` note is waived. Transcripts are cached, so re-running after a fix is instant.
  5. **Desktop: `scripts/script_verify.py "<client folder>"` prints STRICT CHECK OK** (Spencer, 2026-10-28, Funnel S). Why script_check alone let words through: Whisper wrote the script and Whisper checked it, so both dropped the same words, and 1-2 word misses were forgiven (Dark Docs shipped "Hacker on a bullet wound" for "packed around a bullet wound"; AI Revolution "full waits" for "full weights"). The strict check has three independent ears vote: faster-whisper large-v3, openai-whisper medium, and the video's own YouTube captions trimmed to the excerpt window in sources.txt. Any difference two ears agree on fails it, one word included. One-ear differences print as REVIEW (look, don't fail); a name the ears spell their own ways, or spelled as in the video's title/description, prints as SPELLING. A clip that cuts into the next sentence gets a `[... don't voice it.]` note, which waives it. `--funnel "<Funnel dir>"` checks every client. Captions 429 = it runs on two ears and says so; retry later for the third.
  `revoice_build.py`'s `script_gate()` runs script_check AND the strict check on every `_script.docx` of every package, finished ones included, and FAILs the package on a miss. A Google Doc-only script no longer passes unchecked (that's how Airbnb's missing tag line got through as "OK script is a Google Doc"): it reports CHECK, and you export its text (FGAC `drive/v3/files/<id>/export?mimeType=text/plain`) and run `script_check.py --src <source> --text <export>`. **Never convert an existing script to .docx, re-flow it or rewrite it to satisfy the gate** (Spencer, 2026-10-13: the 10-12 conversion "made the scripts worse"; Funnel I was reverted to its Google Docs, and he edits Funnel Q/P himself). A real word error found by the check gets reported to Spencer with the timestamp and the two readings; change the script only when he asks. New cloud scripts are uploaded as `<Client>_script.docx` via rclone. Report each script as ✅ transcript / ❌ missing. One header line (source + "verify against the video") is the only non-copy allowed at the top; notes about the read go *below* the copy.

## Step 5 — Ableton project per video (fully automated, verified 2026-09-11)
`.als` = **gzip-compressed XML**. Copy the template folder to `<Client>/<Client> Project/`, rename the `.als` to `<Client> Project.als`, then patch the two clips named `ORIGINAL VIDEO` and `VIDEO WITHOUT VOX`. Inside each `<AudioClip>` → `<SampleRef>`:
- `<FileRef>`: `RelativePath` = `../<filename>`, `Path` = absolute path with forward slashes, `OriginalFileSize` = real bytes.
- `DefaultDuration` = duration_s × sample_rate; `DefaultSampleRate` from `ffprobe -select_streams a:0 -show_entries stream=sample_rate`.
- Beat-length fields `CurrentEnd`, `LoopEnd`, `OutMarker`, `HiddenLoopEnd`, `ScrollerTimePreserver/RightTime` = **duration_s × 2** (template tempo is fixed 120 BPM manual → 1 beat = 0.5 s).
- Leave `WarpMarkers` alone (template's 2-point linear 120 bpm map is file-independent).
- ORIGINAL VIDEO ← source mp4; VIDEO WITHOUT VOX ← NOVOX mp4. (`Duration` from `ffprobe -show_entries format=duration`.)
- Re-gzip with `gzip.GzipFile(path,"wb",mtime=0)`; `ET.fromstring()` the XML before and after round-trip.
Live Ableton needs nothing else to treat an mp4 as video+audio; a bare `VO` track "Sample Offline" and a "Media files are missing" banner on first open come from template leftovers, not the patched clips. Verified: project opened, Video Window played real frames, status bar showed the exact file path.
Template tracks: `VO Chain` > `VO` (full mix chain), `~VAL/~SHORT/~LMS` refs, `ORIGINAL VIDEO`, `VIDEO WITHOUT VOX`, returns, `Main`.

## Step 7 — FINAL QC (mandatory, last step of every run, on every client touched — Spencer, 2026-10-02)

**Why this exists:** on 2026-10-02 Spencer went to use Funnel G (marked "complete" on 09-30/10-01) and found it wasn't usable — no hooks, no line referencing the re-voice in the pitch email, an Ableton project that wasn't actually wired up. The failure was that "done" meant *a file with the right name exists in the folder* — nobody had opened any of the files to check what was actually inside them. A directory listing is not QC. **Never report a client "done" on file existence alone.** For every client this run touched, before the digest goes out:

1. **Script doc** — open it (don't just confirm the filename exists) and re-run the Step 4 script gate: narrator's lines in order, no placeholder text, and **`script_check.py` must print SCRIPT CHECK OK against that exact source video** (Spencer, 2026-10-12, after Airbnb shipped without its tag line: "Make the script writing fool proof. This is the easiest part of the process there isn't a reason why there should be faults in the transcription."). A word count or a caption read is not proof. FAILED = fix the script from the printed gaps (the video is the truth), re-run until OK; never report a package done without it. Report it in the checklist as `script ✓ (SCRIPT CHECK OK)`.
2. **Ableton project** — download the `.als`, `gzip.decompress()` it, and grep the decompressed XML for `<SampleRef>` → `<FileRef>` blocks. Confirm there are exactly two, and that both `Path` and `RelativePath` point to **this client's own** source and NOVOX filenames (not a leftover template reference, not another client's file, not empty). A `.als` existing in the folder proves nothing by itself — only the decompressed content does.
2b. **MAKE VIDEO button (Spencer, 2026-10-05: "you have to be thorough... I shouldn't have to be waiting")** — `Ready to Link to Drafted Email/MAKE VIDEO - double-click me.bat` must exist, carry this client's name in its `title` line, and call `mux_take.py`. `revoice_build.py` writes it automatically; a package built any other way (hand-built, cloud) gets it written before it's called done. Missing button = package NOT done (Funnel G2 shipped without it on 10-05). **Two+ videos in one client folder:** each button passes its own WAV (`"%~dp0<Client> N Project.wav"`) AND its own source video to `mux_take.py`, never the "newest WAV" default, or button 1 grabs project 2's export (Nat Geo, 10-05). Model: `Funnel G2/National Geographic/Ready to Link to Drafted Email/`. **Cuts made in Ableton carry over (Spencer, 2026-10-14, Vyond: "edit the video and the audio with certain cuts, only export the WAV"):** `mux_take.py` reads the saved `.als` and rebuilds the picture clip for clip from the ORIGINAL VIDEO track (trims, cuts, moves; gaps go black), then lays the WAV on top. An untouched project still stream-copies. Spencer saves the set, exports the WAV only (from 1.1.1) and double-clicks; never Ableton's video export (it froze the first 25 s of Vyond). The run prints the cut list and a CHECK line if the WAV length doesn't match the edit (set not saved).
3. **Gmail draft** — re-read the LIVE draft (full format, not snippet) right before checking it, since other sessions edit this mailbox concurrently. Confirm:
   - The hook names a real, specific, verifiable detail (not "saw your post, hope it goes great" — that's explicitly banned, 2026-09-29 rule) and that detail doesn't contradict the chosen video (check against the `_candidates` doc's WINNER line — a hook that references a *rejected* candidate is a bug, not a style issue).
   - There is an explicit line naming the specific picked video/spot by title (not generic "one of your videos") with a hyperlink — `https://drive.google.com/REPLACE-WITH-SAMPLE-LINK` as the placeholder href is correct and expected until Spencer actually records his take; a missing sentence is not.
   - No leftover boilerplate ("I'd love to put together a quick custom read... happy to send one over") once the above line has been inserted — that boilerplate is ONLY correct for a client with no package yet (e.g. Psyop).
4. **Report a per-client checklist in the digest**, not just "package built": `script ✓/✗ · ableton ✓/✗ · button ✓/✗ · draft hook ✓/✗ · draft video-line ✓/✗`. A client is only "done" when all five are ✓. Anything less goes in the digest as exactly what's missing, named per client — never folded into a blanket "complete" count.

## Gotchas
- Always verify the real contact/draft state live (Gmail via FGAC) before touching outreach — see CLAUDE.md 2a.
- Funnel letters: A/B hand-built, C = e-learning, D = Shell/Experian/Shelter UK/Purdue/Snowflake/Sarofsky/Tendril/Goalhanger/iAM Learning/Skillsoft (all 10 packages built 2026-09-24); next drafted cohort = next letter.
- Big files (>~20 MB) may fail the Drive API base64 upload; Drive-for-Desktop local copy avoids it.
- **NOVOX MATCH GATE (Spencer, 2026-10-07: "This is killing my flow. You have to make sure this doesn't happen again").** The Economist and BUCK shipped with another client's music bed (right picture, wrong audio; BUCK's was even the right length). Root cause: the cloud prompt still used the shared `/tmp/revoice/stems` + `audio.wav` paths below, so parallel builds overwrote each other's `no_vocals.wav`. Now: `scripts/novox_check.py` reads what each `.als` actually plays (ORIGINAL VIDEO + VIDEO WITHOUT VOX) and checks the NOVOX audio is that source's own bed (fast correlation >= 0.30, else a fresh Demucs re-separation must match >= 0.40; right = 0.65-0.99, wrong = 0.01-0.02). It is wired into `revoice_build.py` (every built AND already-complete package; single-video mismatches are rebuilt on the spot, the bad file kept in `_bad_novox/`), `cloud_ableton.py build()` (REFUSES to build on a mismatch) and the cloud prompt Step 2b (per-client paths + NOVOX MATCH OK required). Length match is never proof. Run `python novox_check.py "D:/My Drive/Client Outreach/Funnel X"` on any funnel before Spencer starts recording it.
- **Shared-sandbox Demucs/Whisper collisions (found 2026-10-05, 10 clients built in parallel via subagents):** several concurrent sessions used the literal example filename `audio.wav` with the shared default output dir `/tmp/revoice/stems/htdemucs/audio/` — a second client's job silently overwrote the first's `vocals.wav`/`no_vocals.wav` (caught by a duration mismatch on the output, not by anything in the pipeline itself). **Always use a client-unique input filename and output dir** (e.g. `/tmp/revoice/<Client>/<client>_audio.wav` → `-o /tmp/revoice/<Client>/stems_out`), never the bare `audio.wav`/`stems` example path, whenever more than one client build may run at once. Heavy concurrent load (6–10 simultaneous demucs/whisper jobs) also stretched normal run times a lot (demucs 15–60s clip → 15–35 min; Whisper medium → up to 45 min) and can get killed by a tool's own background time limit — `nohup ... & disown` into a per-client log file survives that.
- **Script-gate exception for creative/lyrical copy (2026-10-05, Psyop's Fage "Plain Extraordinary"):** the word-for-word rule is for ordinary descriptive/explainer ad copy. A spot whose copy is itself a complete, named, award-winning creative work (rhyming verse, a sung jingle, etc.) is the one case where reproducing it in full in the script doc isn't appropriate — leave a structural description + the source link instead and have Spencer transcribe the short verse by ear. Don't extend this exception to plain prose narration (explainer/product-overview copy) just because it's long — see The Economist 2026-10-05, a 213-word factual explainer script, which got the full word-for-word treatment as normal.
