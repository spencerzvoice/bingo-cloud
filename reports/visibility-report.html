The six moves, in order

A. Get onto European agency rosters and UK voice directories

The biggest move, and it's also lane 2 (representation) for Europe, separate from DDO in the US. Every gap above except your name is won by a roster page. Bingo researches each site (free listing, paid listing, or agency submission, and what they ask for), then drafts submissions for your OK. Targets: The Voice Realm, Voice Squad, QVoice, Voice Fox, Great British Voices, The Voiceover Gallery, London Voiceover, Nordling's Talent, Damn Good Voices, Soho Voices, Speaker-Search, Voicfy, Native Voiceover. Free listings and agency submissions only; anything paid comes to you as a yes/no.

B. Third-party proof the "best" answers look for

Fuller wins "best" on a One Voice Award and an agency CV. Your counter: reviews on Google Business Profile, Voices.com, Voice123, Bodalgo and VoiceBros (asked by email, drafted for you), and a decision on entering the One Voice Awards 2027 (paid entry, so your call; dates to be checked).

C. Earned links: press, podcasts, client credit pages

Raises Google's trust in the whole domain, which every Google gap needs. Qwoted pitches on the working-traveller story (2–3 a week), podcast guest spots, and credit links from Noah Media (FIFA) and clients with portfolio pages. Each one goes out only with your OK.

D. Site fixes (Bingo, live on the site)

1. Find out in Search Console why Google shows /es/ and /e-learning/ for your name (the language tags are correct), then strengthen the homepage as the page about you. 2. Add UK-facing proof to /american-voice-over-europe/: Lisbon runs on London time, so live sessions happen during UK office hours. Credits on it only where the client is verified as UK-based. 3. Link your GFTB page from the site so Brave finds it.

E. Profiles as ranking pages

Perplexity and Gemini pull from Voices.com and Voice123 profiles. Check that every profile links to spencerzvoice.com and that its headline says "American male voice over, Lisbon, Europe". Bingo does the audit; edits to marketplace profiles get your OK first.

The "Narrated by Spencer Pearman · spencerzvoice.com" line on every video you voice: a name signal for the AI tools, though YouTube links don't lift Google rankings. Plus a Google Analytics property for spencerzvoice.com, so we can see what traffic the rankings bring.
