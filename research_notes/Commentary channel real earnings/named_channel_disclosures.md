# Real, publicly disclosed earnings of celebrity-downfall / pop-culture commentary YouTube channels

_Researched 2026-10-30. Channel scale figures (subs / videos / views / country) were read directly from each channel's YouTube About page on 2026-10-30 unless stated otherwise. vidIQ MCP had no credits, so no vidIQ API data was used; "vidIQ" figures below are from its public stats pages as quoted in search results. Every money figure is tagged **(a) creator disclosure**, **(b) third-party estimate** or **(c) rumour/unverified**. Nothing in (b) is real earnings._

## Key question 1 — Which of these creators have shown or stated real AdSense numbers (RPM, monthly revenue, per-video revenue)?

### Takeaway
Only three of the named channels have put real numbers on the record: MoistCr1TiKaL (lifetime YouTube ~$35.9M, ~97% from watch-page ads, Oct 2025), Spill Sesh (~$20k/month average YouTube ads, best months ~$50k, Nov 2023) and Internet Anarchist (one 1.5M-view video ≈ $8,000, April 2023). Patrick Cc and Coffeezilla have described their income mix qualitatively but never given dollar figures; SunnyV2, Turkey Tom, Nerd City, The Right Opinion and Moon have disclosed nothing I could find.

## Key question 3 — What RPM does celebrity/pop-culture commentary actually get vs other niches, and what do creators say about limited ads, yellow icons and Content ID?

### Takeaway
There is no creator-published RPM for this niche; the implied figures from the two usable disclosures are ~$5/1,000 (Internet Anarchist, 2023) and ~$3/1,000 lifetime (MoistCr1TiKaL). YouTube's own policy says the "controversial issues" rules apply "even if the content is purely commentary" and that focusing on sex scandals earns no ads, while non-graphic news coverage and crime documentaries can monetize. Commentary channels (incl. Turkey Tom) were hit by a mass "Limited" wave in July 2020, and Internet Anarchist's $8k Content ID case is the clearest documented revenue loss.

### Cited Findings
- Business Insider's aggregate of creator-shared figures (via Yahoo, updated 5 May 2023): RPMs "ranged between $1.61 and $29.30 per 1,000 views" across 8 YouTubers; monthly earnings between $82 and $83,000 across 26; no commentary creator named — [Yahoo / Business Insider](https://tech.yahoo.com/business/articles/much-money-youtube-stars-earn-204224482.html)
- (b) OutlierKit niche estimates (vendor, "for planning", not a creator survey): commentary & reaction drama $3–8; celebrity drama & news $3–6; YouTube creator drama $2–5; US/UK/CA/AU audiences 2–3× global average; says creator-drama brand safety is "low" and coverage with controversy or certain language gets flagged as limited ads — [OutlierKit](https://outlierkit.com/resources/youtube-drama-channel-rpm-cpm/)
- YouTube policy: "Controversial issues" (abuse, self-harm, etc.) "may apply even if the content is purely commentary"; "Non-graphic, main topic news coverage of controversial issues" can earn ad revenue; "A passing reference to a controversial issue is not considered focus on that issue"; "Documentaries about crime" can earn; "Focus on sex scandals or the leaking of private intimate material" = no ad earnings; moderate profanity in title/thumbnail = limited ads; "Please note that context is very important" — [YouTube Help: Advertiser-friendly content guidelines](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/6162278)
- July 2020: TheQuartering, CreepShowArt, Joey Saladino, Bowblax, Turkey Tom, Drunken Peasants and Nicholas DeOrio had monetized videos flipped to "Limited" status (ineligible for most ad types); creators said ad revenue was their only income; YouTube's replies were inconsistent; no percentage loss quoted — [Reclaim The Net, 2 Jul 2020](https://reclaimthenet.org/youtube-creators-another-wave-of-mass-demonetization)
- Nerd City's 2019 test: "gay" and "abortion" consistently demonetized as titles while "abortions" was monetized; results called arbitrary — [Tubefilter](https://www.tubefilter.com/2019/09/30/nerd-city-demonetization-transparency-lgbtq-study/)
- Coffeezilla / Armchair Historian (2019): demonetization is "video suppression" (Armchair Historian); losing one video can be "an entire month's worth of income" for a monthly uploader; sponsors decide on expected views, so suppression also costs sponsor deals — [transcript](https://rosetta.to/u/coffeezilla/demonetization-conversation-armchair-historian)
- Content ID / claims: Internet Anarchist lost ~$8,000 on a 1.5M-view video to a third-party claim (Thumb Media for Adin Ross), reinstated after public pressure — [Sportskeeda](https://www.sportskeeda.com/esports/news-youtuber-discloses-losing-8-000-revenue-adin-ross-allegedly-falsely-claimed-video); a claim lets the claimant collect the video's revenue, and three strikes terminate a channel — [Passionfruit](https://passionfru.it/adin-ross-copyright-strikes-4770/)
- Internet Anarchist again (Dec 2025): gave 100% of a plagiarised video's revenue to the original creator — [Plagiarism Today](https://www.plagiarismtoday.com/2026/01/05/the-shocking-downfall-of-the-internet-anarchist/)
- (b) Generic explainers put the limited-ads revenue hit at "50 to 90%" or "20 to 90%", unsourced — [Thumblore](https://www.thumblore.com/blogs/youtube-limited-ads-yellow-icon)

### Inferences
- The IA figure (~$5.3/1,000 views on a Sneako/Cr1TiKaL drama video, 2023, mostly US/UK audience) sits inside OutlierKit's $3–8 commentary band, which is the only cross-check available.
- Internet Anarchist's channel is based in Australia but its subject matter and the $8k-per-1.5M-views suggest a US/UK-weighted audience; no audience geography was published.

### Gaps
- No creator in this lane has posted a YouTube Studio RPM screenshot. No statement from SunnyV2, Patrick Cc, Turkey Tom or Nerd City about yellow-icon rates on their own videos was found.
- Spill Sesh's $20k/month cannot be converted to RPM (no monthly view figure in the NYT/TIME coverage).

## Key question 4 — Disclosures from faceless downfall channels about the business model (writer/editor/VO costs, profit per video)?

Nothing quantified. ### Inferences
- The faceless downfall channels that matter (Internet Anarchist, SunnyV2) run as small studios with outsourced writing; what they pay writers, editors or narrators is not public anywhere I could find.

### Gaps
- No writer/editor/VO rate, cost per video or margin disclosed by Moon, Internet Anarchist or SunnyV2.

- vidIQ MCP had no credits, so per-video view averages for the last 30 days could not be pulled; YouTube's upload lists are client-rendered and could not be scraped.
