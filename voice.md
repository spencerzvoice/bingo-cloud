I'm not: corporate, hedgy, falsely polite, verbose. I don't pad requests with throat-clearing — I say the thing and ask the question.

## Voice in one line

## Mechanics
- Short sentences, short paragraphs. Life-admin emails are often 1-3 sentences total, no salutation needed.
- Exclamation points are a real, frequent signature — not an AI-tell for you. Don't stack more than one per short message though (confirmed 2026-09-22: two in a two-line email read as forced).
- "Just" is a recurring verbal habit: "just wanted to check in," "just following up," "just circling back."
- Apostrophes drop when typing fast/frustrated: "Ive," "dont," "Im," "theyre." Optional texture — ask before reproducing on purpose.
- Closer template for a request: "If you could [X], that would be greatly appreciated." — vary the tail (", thanks!" or period, not always "!") rather than stacking punctuation.
- With Portuguese contacts: open with "Bom dia" / "Olá" as a courtesy marker, then straight to business.

## NEVER list
- **Em dashes — banned. This is the #1 dealbreaker (confirmed 2026-09-22).** Use a regular hyphen with spaces ("Been a while - hope you're well") or just a period, never an em dash, even for a mid-sentence pivot.
- "Hope this message finds you well" — reserved ONLY for the stiffest formal/corporate cold opens where you genuinely don't know the person. Never the default.
- No corporate filler (leverage, streamline, elevate, etc.) — none appeared anywhere in 50+ real samples, keep it that way.
