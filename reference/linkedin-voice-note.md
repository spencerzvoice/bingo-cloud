# LinkedIn voice note — after a connection accept

Rule (Spencer, 2026-10-16): **no pitch, no hook.** A voice note is a hello and a thank-you. Never mention their post, a campaign, a launch, a re-voice title or credits. ("We're not hooking. We're not doing that.")

Send it right away when they accept. Don't hold it for Monday or a better local hour; an accept is interest (Spencer, 10-16: "This isn't one we wait on").

## Script (about 10–15 s)
**Already emailed:**
> "Hey <First>, Spencer here. Thanks for connecting! I sent you an email a couple weeks ago. Would love to work with you. Have a great day!"

**Never emailed** (no email history, e.g. a local creative):
> "Hey <First>, Spencer here. Thanks for connecting! Would love to work with you sometime. Have a great day!"

**Accepted before the pitch went out** (Spencer asked 10-26; Bingo's recommendation):
> "Hey <First>, Spencer here, I'm a voiceover artist based in Lisbon. Thanks for connecting! Would love to work with you sometime. Have a great day!"
- Send it right away, same as any accept. One identity line is allowed because they don't know him yet; still no credits, hook or re-voice.
- Don't mention the coming email ("I'll be sending you something"): it reads as a sales setup, and it's a promise that looks bad if the pitch slips or the contact changes.
- The pitch goes out on its normal schedule, word for word from PITCH-TEMPLATE.md (no LinkedIn mention added). If it's already scheduled for the next day or two, that's fine.
- The voice note counts as contact: note it in the tracker, and touch 2's no-contact check rolls from the email, not the voice note.

Adjust "a couple weeks ago" to the real gap (check Gmail Sent for the date). Portuguese contacts: the same message in pt-PT.

After it's sent: tracker Notes + Last Contact, and no more LinkedIn messages; the next touch is by email or their reply.
