# Funnel S research, Lane A: consumer tech / AI / internet and company history / business of tech
_Researched 2026-10-26. Read-only. Nothing sent, no drafts made._

**Bottom line:** in this lane, most faceless tech channels between 400K and 2M are narrated by their founders. I confirmed that from the founders' own words in the transcripts (Logically Answered, Apple Explained, Company Man, Hoog, TheAIGRID, Species, Visual Venture). Only **3 channels have documented paid narrators**: Rational Animations, Fern and DidYouKnowGaming. Each has a catch. Three more are open questions (❓) worth one listen or one email: Modern MBA, AI Revolution and Moon.

**Tag key:** ✅ = I opened the primary source this session (named). 🟡 = unverified. ❓ = unknown.
**Sources used:** subscriber counts and upload dates come from yt-dlp on the channel's /videos tab on 2026-10-26 ✅. Descriptions also come from yt-dlp. "Transcript" means YouTube's own auto-captions, pulled with yt-dlp and read for self-identification.

**Ranking rule (from the coordinator):** a paid narrator ranks first, then a 1–2 week cadence ahead of monthly, then sponsor reads, then fit with the lane.
