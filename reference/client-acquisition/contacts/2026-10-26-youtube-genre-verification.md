# YouTube narrator market check: 8 genres (2026-10-26)

**Method.** Subscriber counts and the last 5 upload dates come from yt-dlp on each channel's /videos tab, run this session, so they are ✅. Each narrator call cites its evidence:
- (D) a credit in a video description, pulled with yt-dlp this session ✅
- (C) the video's own caption track: self-intros ("I'm Hari", "My name is Jake"), first-person lines, and sponsor reads ✅. Captions show WHAT is said. They can't show WHOSE voice it is, unless the narrator names himself.
- (A) the channel's About text, pulled with yt-dlp ✅
- (S) a search-engine summary only. The source page was not opened, so it is 🟡.

**Format** is judged from first-person captions, titles and credits. I didn't watch any video frames, so "on camera" is 🟡 unless a credit says "Hosted by".

**Range.** The brief was 400k–5M subscribers. A few channels just outside it are listed and marked OUT.

**Where this is weak.** I couldn't open IMDb or Upwork (403, and a curl workaround was blocked). Every IMDb narrator credit below is therefore 🟡.
