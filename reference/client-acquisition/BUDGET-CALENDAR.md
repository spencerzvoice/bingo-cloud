# Budget calendar — when each company's money moves

**Purpose:** time outreach to each company's funding and campaign schedule, so the pitch lands while a buyer is writing next year's plan, spending fresh budget, or (for vendors already on file) spending down. Timing decides whose shortlist Spencer is on when a booking happens. It never decides whether a draft gets written.

_Last updated: 2026-10-05 (built by Bingo from `ROSTER.md`, `FUNNEL-COHORTS.md`, `memory/followups-queue.md`, `memory/outreach-log.md`, and `reports/Campaign budgets and VO timing.md`)._

**Tag key**
- ✅ = primary source opened this session, named inline. For fiscal years this is almost always **SEC EDGAR 2026-10-05**: the live `data.sec.gov/submissions/CIK##########.json` record, field `fiscalYearEnd`, queried by script on 2026-10-05, with tickers mapped through `sec.gov/files/company_tickers.json`. Two non-US ones came from investor-relations pages opened the same day (Siemens, Hitachi).
- 🟡 = unverified: an archetype default, an inference, a parent-company link not checked this session, or a fact relayed from a repo file without reopening it.
- ❓ = unknown. Private companies don't publish a fiscal year, so they get "private — ❓ FY not public" and their archetype's default timing (🟡). No FY was guessed.

**Read the FY column carefully**
- "(Parent)" means the FY is the parent company's from EDGAR; the subsidiary relationship itself is 🟡 unless stated.
- "last 10-K, pre-take-private; may have changed ❓" means EDGAR shows a delisting (form 25-NSE / 15-12G) and the FY is from the final public filing. Owners can change the year after a buyout.
- EDGAR records for foreign companies that only have ADR paperwork (F-6), such as Siemens Energy, Experian and Sophos, carry a `fiscalYearEnd` that is not an annual-report fact. Those were **not** used as ✅.
- 52/53-week filers (Cisco, YETI, Garmin, lululemon, Disney) show that year's actual period end, which moves a few days each year.
- Ownership says "ticker X" where the ticker was matched in EDGAR's ticker file. Exchange names and countries for non-SEC filers are 🟡.
- **Status / Funnel is a snapshot** copied from ROSTER, FUNNEL-COHORTS and followups-queue on 2026-10-05. It is **not** live Gmail. Per CLAUDE.md 2a, check live Gmail (FGAC) before drafting, sending or deleting anything.

**How to use**
1. **Lead Batch** adds a row here for every new company at research time, in the same run: ownership, archetype, FY end (US-listed: EDGAR `fiscalYearEnd` ✅; private: ❓ plus the archetype default 🟡, never guessed), new-money month, and best pitch windows.
2. **followup-drafts** uses the row to pick the best date *inside* a quarterly long-tail (LT) window. Example: an LT1 window of Jan 5–20 for a Jan-31 FY company should go early (Jan 5–16, FY-end spend), and a calendar-FY company should go from Jan 12 on.
3. **Nothing here overrides the blackouts:** no cold sends in US Thanksgiving week (Nov 23–27 2026), none Dec 14 2026 – Jan 4 2027, and none to continental-EU contacts in August. Light sending only Nov 30 – Dec 11 (trigger-led sends, plus warm notes to vendors already on file).
4. Timing informs. It never blocks a draft. Final VO is cast in 1–3 days from a shortlist of 1–5 voices (report, Kim Handysides 2021, per the report and not reopened), so the aim is to be in the pool before the money and the launch arrive, not to hit a casting week.
