## Why the order

**MasterClass is not a close call for first place.** A 23 September sweep returned fourteen production and creative staff: a Post Production Manager, a Senior Manager of Post Production & Operations, three Executive Producers, a Showrunner, a VP Executive Creative Director, a Head of Creative, and six producers. That is a television-scale organisation buying narration continuously, and no other company in the funnel has that bench depth.

**Post Production Manager remains the title to chase** — post supervision owns the audio chain, so the person holding it books and directs the voice. Three of the top ten have one (MasterClass, Skillshare, and LearningMate outside this list).

**2U is the edX door.** edX returns no production staff of its own because 2U owns it. One conversation at 2U reaches both, and 2U carries three creative directors (Toby Ridley; Waide Grandison, CD Multimedia–Marketing; Annie Johnson, CD Copy) plus three video producers. This is the single best unworked opportunity in the funnel.

**Berklee Online has a non-generic angle.** Six creative staff including an **Audio** Creative Producer (Andres Gomez-Garcia), a Senior Director of Creative Media, and a Video Producer & Online Course Developer. Spencer is a musician with sync-licensing interests — that is a real point of contact rather than a manufactured one, and it is the rare case where the personal background is the hook.

**IxDF is the deliberate exception to the marketing-only rule.** Batch 10 flagged that Roman Kurachenko's Head of Video title likely covers IxDF's actual course production — which *is* the product. Their mid-2026 platform release rewrote their content playbook to add "lots more animations, real-world footage and on-screen text." Course narration is a larger and steadier volume than the usual Funnel C marketing angle, so this one is worth breaking the rule for.

**Domestika** is Spanish-first and expanding into English — precisely where an American voice gets bought. Content Production Manager plus a Post-Production Lead and a co-founder running Creative & Design.

## Method note worth keeping

The 3 September sweep concluded that Articulate, Docebo, 360Learning, Cornerstone, Absorb and TalentLMS were empty. That was a false negative caused by searching production titles only. Re-running with marketing and creative titles turned zero usable contacts into 275 hits. Batch 08 later found Cornerstone OnDemand does have a Lead Video Producer after all — headcount growth, not a contradiction.

**Any Funnel C or D sweep must run both title sets:** production titles (`Post Production Manager`, `Video Producer`, `Creative Producer`, `Head of Video`, `Content Production Manager`) and marketing/creative titles (`Creative Director`, `Head of Brand`, `Head of Content`, `Head of Creative Operations`, `Content Marketing Manager`).
