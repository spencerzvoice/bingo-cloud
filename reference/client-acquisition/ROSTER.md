## Method notes worth keeping

**Post Production Manager is the single strongest title signal.** Post supervision owns the audio chain, so whoever holds it books and directs the voice.

**Funnels C and D require two title sets.** The 3 Sep sweep concluded Articulate, Docebo, 360Learning, Cornerstone, Absorb and TalentLMS were empty. That was a false negative from searching production titles only. Re-running with marketing and creative titles turned zero usable contacts into 275 hits.

- Production titles: `Post Production Manager`, `Video Producer`, `Creative Producer`, `Head of Video`, `Content Production Manager`
- Marketing/creative titles: `Creative Director`, `Head of Brand`, `Head of Content`, `Head of Creative Operations`, `Content Marketing Manager`

**A verified email is not proof of a current job.** Enrichment has repeatedly caught people who had already moved on — Angela Branch (Descript → Harvey), Ian Wiggins (Siemens → Hitachi Rail), two M2 Film contacts verified-but-departed. A first-party team page beats a verified mailbox. This matters most in Funnel J, where studio staff churn fastest.

**Two Gmail accounts exist.** `spencerzpearman@gmail.com` is personal; `spencer@spencerzvoice.com` is the VO business account and the only one that matters. A run on 11 Sep checked the wrong one, found it empty, and drafted nine emails into the personal account.
