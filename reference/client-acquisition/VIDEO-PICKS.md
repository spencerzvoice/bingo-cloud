# Re-voice video picks: what works, what doesn't

Spencer asked (2026-10-02) for a running log of good and bad source-video picks, so picks get better over time. **Read this before choosing any re-voice video** (Re-Voicing routine Stage 1, `revoice-material` skill Step 1). Add a line every time Spencer reacts to a pick, records one, or rejects one.

## Hard rules (Spencer)
- **No VO that belongs to a woman on screen** (2026-10-02). A female *announcer* is fine. Reject: a woman on camera as talking head/presenter/speaking lead, or a female VO that is the on-screen woman's own voice (inner monologue, first-person "I/my" while she's the character we follow).
- **No talking heads or lip-sync**, any gender. Spencer's voice can't replace a mouth we're watching.
- **Single narrator.** No dialogue, interviews, multi-voice montages, or sung copy.
- Gate: `scripts/narrator_check.py` (voice pitch + contact sheet) on every winner. Look at the sheet.

## What makes a good pick (patterns so far)
- Off-camera announcer over b-roll, graphics, aerials or product shots.
- 15 to 60 s, a real brand ad (TV/web spot), not a promo tie-in or novelty piece.
- 720p or better, ideally from the brand's own channel or site.
- Copy with a clear arc and a closing line Spencer can land.
- Recent (last ~3 years) beats old, but a clean old spot beats a messy new one.

## What makes a bad pick
- Woman on camera speaking, or her voice as the VO (Coinbase "15 bank visits").
- Novelty/tie-in promos that aren't really ads for the core product (DraftKings "Love Love" fragrance promo).
- Low-res sources (360p) and thin scripts where the visuals carry the spot (Instacart "Apartment Farm", 18 words). Aim for narration that runs the whole length, roughly 2+ words/sec.
- Celebrity on camera carrying the spot (most DraftKings/Kevin Hart ads).
